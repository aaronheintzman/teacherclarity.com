Leadership & Experience

Teacher Clarity is part of Clarity in School, led by its Managing Partner, whose professional background includes years of developing educational partnerships, supporting professional learning initiatives, and working alongside school and district leaders.

That experience includes supporting hundreds of professional learning engagements annually, developing multi-school and districtwide partnerships, and coordinating educational consultants across a wide range of instructional and organizational priorities.

Having worked with schools, districts, county offices of education, and educational organizations across California and other states, the leadership behind Teacher Clarity brings a practical understanding of the challenges educational organizations face and the importance of connecting educators with meaningful resources and expertise.
